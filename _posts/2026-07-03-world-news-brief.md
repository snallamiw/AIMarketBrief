**Iran opens a historic multi-day state funeral for assassinated Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, even as US-Iran ceasefire talks in Doha stall and a massive Russian strike leaves Kyiv burying its dead.**

### World News

**Iran: Khamenei funeral begins.** The casket of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, killed in a joint US-Israeli strike on February 28, was carried into Tehran's Grand Mosalla Friday for a two-day public farewell, opening seven days of processions across Tehran, Qom, Najaf, Karbala and Mashhad, ending with burial at the Imam Reza shrine on July 9. Iranian officials expect 15-20 million mourners and delegations from more than 100 countries, making it the largest state funeral in Iran's history. Why it matters: security services have mobilized the Basij amid fears of unrest or further attacks; the funeral is also a proxy for who consolidates power in post-Khamenei Iran. Watch for: crowd security incidents and whether the funeral affects the pace of nuclear talks. ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/international/5951099-khamenei-funeral-massive-crowds/), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/world/khamenei-body-cold-storage-feared-basij-mobilizes-historic-iran-funeral))

**Iran-US talks stall in Doha.** The two sides are operating under a June 17 memorandum extending their ceasefire 60 days. Qatari mediators, US envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met in Doha this week; technical talks concluded without a breakthrough, though both sides agreed on interim steps, including tapping part of $6 billion in Iranian assets frozen in Qatar for supplies. Iran says a Lebanon ceasefire is a precondition for broader talks and has ruled out negotiating its ballistic missile program. Why it matters: the ceasefire is holding but fragile, and unresolved issues (Hormuz, sanctions, enrichment stockpiles) remain live flashpoints. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/1/us-iran-negotiations-whats-the-latest), [Congress.gov CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12678))

**Ukraine: deadliest Kyiv strike in months.** An 11-hour Russian barrage of 74 missiles and 496 drones hit Kyiv overnight, killing at least 30 and injuring 113+; a nine-story apartment building partially collapsed in the Darnytskyi district, and 50,000+ people sheltered in subway stations. Ukrainian air defenses intercepted 524 of 570 aerial weapons. Why it matters: the scale marks an escalation and followed Ukrainian strikes on Russia's oil sector. [ONGOING]. ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/heavy-russian-strikes-kill-at-least-21-in-kyiv-after-ukrainian-strikes-disrupt-moscows-oil-sector), [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/07/03/8042186/))

**Venezuela earthquake toll climbs.** A week after twin magnitude 7.2/7.5 quakes, Venezuela's government puts the death toll at 2,295 with 11,000+ injured — likely an undercount, per the UN, which is procuring 10,000 body bags. Nearly 59,000 buildings are damaged or destroyed, with damages estimated at $6.7 billion and up to 6.8 million people affected. Experts say Venezuela's dysfunctional government response is deepening the crisis. ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/in-venezuela-a-completely-ineffective-government-worsens-earthquake-disaster-experts-say), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/01/nx-s1-5877369/venezuela-earthquakes-updates))

**Syria: Damascus cafe bombing.** An IED detonated in a popular Damascus cafe, killing five; security forces are hunting the attackers amid Syria's fragile post-war security environment. ### US News

**Supreme Court upholds birthright citizenship.** In a 6-3 ruling (Trump v. Barbara) on June 30, the Court struck down Trump's executive order seeking to end birthright citizenship for children of undocumented or temporary residents, holding it conflicts with the 14th Amendment's Citizenship Clause. Chief Justice Roberts wrote the majority opinion; Kavanaugh concurred in the result on narrower grounds. Why it matters: it forecloses, for now, a central piece of the administration's immigration agenda. ([SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/06/supreme-court-strikes-down-trumps-order-ending-birthright-citizenship/))

**Weak jobs report.** The economy added just 57,000 jobs in June with wage growth remaining slow — a soft signal on labor-market momentum. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/june-jobs-report-stable-hiring-rcna352603))
