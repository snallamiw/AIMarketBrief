## 8. Dividends vs. Capital Gains

| | Dividend | Long-Term Capital Gain |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Company pays you while holding | You sell for a profit |
| Tax rate | Qualified: same as LT cap gains (0/15/20%). Ordinary: your income tax rate | 0%, 15%, or 20% |

**Qualified dividend rule:** Must hold 61+ days within the 121-day window around the ex-dividend date.
- SCHD/VYM/VIG/NKE → mostly qualified
- JEPI/JEPQ/QYLD/RYLD/XYLD → mostly ordinary income (options premium income)

## 10. Warren Buffett's Investment Methods

1. **Intrinsic Value / DCF** — estimate future cash flows, discount to present
2. **Economic Moat** — durable competitive advantages (brand, network effects, cost advantage)
3. **Circle of Competence** — only invest in businesses he understands
4. **Margin of Safety** — buy meaningfully below estimated intrinsic value
5. **Management Quality** — honest, rational capital allocators
6. **Owner Earnings** — net income + D&A − maintenance capex (stricter than standard FCF)
7. **Low Debt** — avoids heavy leverage (opposite philosophy from TQQQ/SPXL-style products)

**Buffett's own advice for most investors:** a low-cost S&P 500 index fund (e.g., VOO) is the sensible default for people who don't want to do individual-company research.
